The Gateway

Wale to Church Street.

The user perceives this space as generally safe. High prevalence of coffee establishments (Mojo, Motherland) creates a tourist cluster.

Observation: Delivery bikes move unexpectedly fast here.

Informal Economy

Church to Longmarket.

This is one of the most convivial blocks. Trees line the walkway, but there is a dominance of informal traders.

Insight: Clothing and Fashion make up 72.5% of trader offerings.

[Chart: Trader Product Categories]

Foreshore Gateway

Alpha & Jetty Street.

Pedestrian movement peaks here. 1,024 people recorded entering the Foreshore at Alpha St during the AM peak.

Issue: High volume but weak infrastructure.

Strategic Overview

90% of informal traders are located between Wale and Riebeek. The mall functions as two distinct zones: the active retail heart and the transit-heavy foreshore.
